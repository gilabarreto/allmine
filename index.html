ALLMINE

ABOUT ALLMINE

ALLMINE is a modern-day take on the classic Minesweeper game. Clear the sea without hitting a mine: numbers show how many mines are nearby, and flags help you mark suspicious spots.

Victor Barreto, a developer and retro gaming enthusiast, recreated and developed this game. Click here to learn more about him and explore his other projects.

HOW TO PLAY

Click or tap a sea square to reveal it. Each number tells you how many mines are in the eight surrounding squares. Reveal every safe square to win. Hit a mine and the game ends.

Right-click to place or remove a flag. On a touch screen, tap the flag button first, then tap a square to mark it. Tap the flag button again to return to revealing squares. After placing your last flag, the game automatically returns to revealing squares.

The counter shows how many flags you have left. Tap the face to start a new game. Resizing or rotating the screen may restart the game.

Tap the bomb to cycle through three levels: the original field, two-thirds of the map, and the entire sea. Yellow means level 2; red means level 3. Changing levels starts a new game.
